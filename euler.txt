  EULER'S FORMULA — The Bridge Between Bits and Waves
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  In 1748, Leonhard Euler published what many call the most
  beautiful equation in all of mathematics:

        e^(iθ) = cos(θ) + i·sin(θ)

  Set θ = π and you get the famous identity:

        e^(iπ) + 1 = 0

  Five fundamental constants — e, i, π, 1, 0 — in one equation.
  But this formula is far more than aesthetic elegance.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  WHY IT MATTERS FOR THIS REPO
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Euler's formula says that an EXPONENTIAL (e^(iθ)) and a WAVE
  (cos + i·sin) are the SAME THING, just written differently.

  This is the continuous-world cousin of Carmack's insight:

    CARMACK:  The bit pattern of a float IS a logarithmic integer.
              Read the bits differently → hidden structure.

    EULER:    An exponential IS a rotating wave.
              Write it differently → hidden structure.

  Every topic in this repo flows from that equivalence:

    • FOURIER TRANSFORM
      Decompose any signal into e^(iωt) components.
      Euler's formula is why this works: each component is
      a sine wave at a specific frequency.

    • FFT (COOLEY-TUKEY)
      The twiddle factors W = e^(-i·2π/N) are Euler's formula
      evaluated at N equally spaced angles. The entire FFT is
      built on the symmetry of roots of unity — complex numbers
      evenly spaced on the unit circle.

    • QUANTUM MECHANICS
      A qubit in state |ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩ has complex amplitudes
      α and β. Quantum gates ROTATE these amplitudes using — you
      guessed it — e^(iθ). The Hadamard gate, the phase gate,
      the entire Quantum Fourier Transform: all Euler's formula
      applied to computation.

    • SAMPLING THEOREM
      Shannon proved that you can perfectly reconstruct a
      continuous signal from discrete samples IF you sample
      fast enough. The proof uses Fourier analysis, which uses
      Euler's formula. Bits and waves meet at the sample.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  THE FORMULA, UNPACKED
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  e^(iθ) traces a CIRCLE in the complex plane:

        Im
         │    · (e^(iπ/2) = i)
         │  ·   ·
         │·       · ← unit circle
    ─────┼─────────·── Re
         │·       ·
         │  ·   ·     (e^(0) = 1) →
         │    ·

  At angle θ:
    • The real part is cos(θ)     — the horizontal position
    • The imaginary part is sin(θ) — the vertical position

  As θ increases, the point SPINS around the circle.
  That's a wave. That's a signal. That's a qubit.

  Fourier said: any signal = sum of spinning arrows at different speeds.
  Euler said: each spinning arrow = e^(iωt).

  The Fast Fourier Transform says: compute all N arrows in O(n log n).

  Quantum computing says: let the arrows interfere, and the answer
  emerges from the pattern.
