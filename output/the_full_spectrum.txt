    │     ▼                                                       │

    This IS the pipeline in your phone:
      • Microphone  → ADC (Stage 2)
      • Codec       → FFT/DCT analysis (Stages 3-4)
      • Network     → Noisy channel (Stage 5)
      • Receiver    → Denoising + reconstruction (Stages 6-7)
      • Speaker     → DAC (reverse of Stage 2)
